Who we are

Vaka Systems helps growing businesses move off spreadsheets and onto systems that fit how they work, then makes their data and AI genuinely useful.

We started in 2023 as Zlano Analytics, a data analytics start-up, and grew into Vaka Systems in 2026, adding ERP implementation, IT consultancy and AI to the data work we began with.

Vaka comes from the Shona kuvaka, to build. Our mark is a V made of blocks of code that ends in a green run block: separate parts brought together into one working system.
